# Topic map and visual production

Populate `topics.json` with objects containing a stable `id`, `title`, `chapter`, `takeaway`, and `source_ids`. IDs must be unique. Update `expected_topics` in `project.json` to match the agreed scope.

For each image, record its takeaway, concise copy, metaphor and its limits, program treatment, source IDs, intended path, and review notes. Begin with a small pilot. Preserve generation prompts and record actual dimensions. A chapter introduction should explain why its topics matter and show their sequence.
