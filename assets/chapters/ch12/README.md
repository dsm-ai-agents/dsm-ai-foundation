## Teaching notes

These are pedagogical recommendations, not guaranteed organizational outcomes. The bottleneck illustration is a simplified analogy; real systems may have multiple interacting constraints. Critical judgment includes considering evidence that could disconfirm a preferred answer. Delegation instructions need enforced permissions and review mechanisms. Adoption requires support and workflow redesign; training alone is not sufficient. Human accountability remains even when agents execute actions. Feedback does not guarantee every iteration improves results; evaluate actual outcomes. Data, judgment, and communication are illustrative transferable skills, not an exhaustive list. Future scenarios are explicitly possibilities, not forecasts.
