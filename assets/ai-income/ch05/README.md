## Teaching and review notes

- Each card asks the reader to assess existing domain skill, choose a useful deliverable, and test one small offer.
- Prices shown are explicitly labeled hypotheses. They are not observed market rates, monthly earnings, or take-home pay. Project prices apply to engagements; retainers apply per month; kits apply per sale.
- Beginner means lower technical entry difficulty, not absence of professional knowledge or review obligations.
- All images were visually inspected for text, branding, clipping, price accuracy and misleading claims. No material errors found. UI panels and paperwork in illustrations are schematic, not real client data.
- Flattened PNGs are not editable slide layers. Exact prompts and original generator paths are retained in manifest.json and jobs.json.

## Topic qualifications and context sources

### AI-021 — Estimate follow-up workflow

Buyer: HVAC and plumbing contractors. AI role: Summarize inquiry context and draft personalized follow-up.

Constraint: Use consented contacts; no invented quotes or automatic commitments.

Evidence context: [S10](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s10), [S17](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s17), [S18](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s18). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-022 — Jobsite voice-note reports

Buyer: Small general contractors. AI role: Transcribe and organize work, delays and action items.

Constraint: Safety incidents require immediate existing procedures, not delayed AI summaries.

Evidence context: [S10](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s10), [S17](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s17), [S18](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s18). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-023 — Bid document checklist service

Buyer: Specialty subcontractors. AI role: Extract and classify bid requirements.

Constraint: Do not guarantee bid completeness or provide engineering judgments.

Evidence context: [S10](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s10), [S17](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s17), [S18](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s18). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-024 — Home-service FAQ assistant setup

Buyer: Roofing and landscaping firms. AI role: Retrieve approved answers and collect basic inquiry details.

Constraint: Native website tools compete; no binding prices or unsafe instructions.

Evidence context: [S10](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s10), [S17](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s17), [S18](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s18). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-025 — Maintenance-plan communication kit
