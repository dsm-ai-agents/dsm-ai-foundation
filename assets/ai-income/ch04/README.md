## Teaching and review notes

- Each card asks the reader to assess existing domain skill, choose a useful deliverable, and test one small offer.
- Prices shown are explicitly labeled hypotheses. They are not observed market rates, monthly earnings, or take-home pay. Project prices apply to engagements; retainers apply per month; kits apply per sale.
- Beginner means lower technical entry difficulty, not absence of professional knowledge or review obligations.
- All images were visually inspected for text, branding, clipping, price accuracy and misleading claims. No material errors found. UI panels and paperwork in illustrations are schematic, not real client data.
- Flattened PNGs are not editable slide layers. Exact prompts and original generator paths are retained in manifest.json and jobs.json.

## Topic qualifications and context sources

### AI-016 — Verified listing launch pack

Buyer: Residential listing agents. AI role: Draft from agent-approved property facts.

Constraint: No invented amenities, misleading imagery or discriminatory housing language.

Evidence context: [S05](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s05), [S17](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s17). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-017 — Property inbox triage setup

Buyer: Small property managers. AI role: Classify messages and summarize issues for staff.

Constraint: Emergency messages need reliable human escalation; tenant privacy matters.

Evidence context: [S05](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s05), [S17](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s17). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-018 — Lease abstract preparation

Buyer: Commercial property managers. AI role: Extract clauses into a reviewed schema.

Constraint: Legal interpretation stays with qualified professionals; missed dates are costly.

Evidence context: [S05](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s05), [S17](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s17). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-019 — Tenant welcome micro-kit
