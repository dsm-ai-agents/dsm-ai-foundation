## Teaching and decision notes

All prices are proposed test prices, not market-rate evidence or earned income. Retainers are per client per month; projects and downloads are one-time sales. These slides help a reader assess existing skills and choose a small test. They do not validate demand or promise revenue. Skills, delivery-hour assumptions, acquisition routes and detailed constraints remain in the full research cards.

- **AI-071 (Intermediate):** No invented grants or funding guarantees; verify each funder's live rules. Assumed 8 delivery/support hours per unit, excluding selling and setup.
- **AI-072 (Intermediate):** Do not fabricate outcomes, beneficiary stories or causal impact. Assumed 26 delivery/support hours per unit, excluding selling and setup.
- **AI-073 (Beginner):** Protect donor data; no automatic solicitation or manipulative claims. Assumed 6 delivery/support hours per unit, excluding selling and setup.
- **AI-074 (Beginner):** Safeguarding and eligibility rules stay with the organization. Assumed 1 delivery/support hours per unit, excluding selling and setup.
- **AI-075 (Intermediate):** Association may already have free sources; editorial expertise differentiates. Assumed 12 delivery/support hours per unit, excluding selling and setup.
