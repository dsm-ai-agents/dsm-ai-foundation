## Teaching and review notes

- Each card asks the reader to assess existing domain skill, choose a useful deliverable, and test one small offer.
- Prices shown are explicitly labeled hypotheses. They are not observed market rates, monthly earnings, or take-home pay. Project prices apply to engagements; retainers apply per month; kits apply per sale.
- Beginner means lower technical entry difficulty, not absence of professional knowledge or review obligations.
- All images were visually inspected for text, branding, clipping, price accuracy and misleading claims. No material errors found. UI panels and paperwork in illustrations are schematic, not real client data.
- Flattened PNGs are not editable slide layers. Exact prompts and original generator paths are retained in manifest.json and jobs.json.

## Topic qualifications and context sources

### AI-026 — Client document chase system

Buyer: Bookkeeping practices. AI role: Classify incoming documents and tailor requests.

Constraint: Do not ingest confidential records into unapproved tools.

Evidence context: [S06](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s06), [S03](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s03). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-027 — Monthly close narrative service

Buyer: Fractional CFOs. AI role: Explain verified variances and draft questions for review.

Constraint: No unsupervised accounting conclusions or invented business explanations.

Evidence context: [S06](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s06), [S03](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s03). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-028 — Receipt extraction exception desk

Buyer: Bookkeepers handling messy records. AI role: OCR and classify fields for human approval.

Constraint: Existing accounting software competes; never auto-post uncertain entries.

Evidence context: [S06](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s06), [S03](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s03). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-029 — Accounting SOP documentation

Buyer: Small CPA firms. AI role: Transcribe and organize procedures and screenshots.

Constraint: Tax guidance and technical judgments require practitioner approval.

Evidence context: [S06](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s06), [S03](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s03). These sources do not validate the proposed price.

### AI-030 — Audit evidence index preparation

Buyer: Small audit practices. AI role: Classify supplied documents and draft index descriptions.

Constraint: Administrative support only; no audit opinion or fabricated evidence.

Evidence context: [S06](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s06), [S03](../../../research/100-ai-income-opportunities/SOURCES.md#s03). These sources do not validate the proposed price.
