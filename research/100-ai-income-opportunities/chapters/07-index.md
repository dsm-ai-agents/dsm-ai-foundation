## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Legal operations experience, source citation, confidentiality and attorney review**.

Potential buyer access: Law-firm operations referrals and legal technology service providers.

Evidence: Medium: sector use supported; limited to supervised professional workflows. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-031: Matter intake organization

**Useful outcome:** Approved intake summary and missing-document checklist.

**Pause if:** No legal advice, conflict determinations or autonomous acceptance of matters.

**First test:** Shadow ten redacted intakes reviewed by an attorney.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-031) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-032: Chronology preparation service

**Useful outcome:** Source-linked timeline of supplied emails and records.

**Pause if:** Critical facts and privilege boundaries require human verification.

**First test:** Compare one small file against an attorney-reviewed chronology.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 24 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-032) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-033: Law-firm knowledge library cleanup

**Useful outcome:** Tagged precedent inventory with owners and update dates.

**Pause if:** Do not reuse confidential documents across clients.

**First test:** Organize one practice-area folder and test retrieval.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 24 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-033) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-034: Client status update drafting

**Useful outcome:** Weekly draft updates based only on approved matter status.

**Pause if:** Attorney approves every message; deadlines and strategy cannot be inferred.

**First test:** Run draft-only updates for five matters for a month.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-034) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-035: Legal AI workflow evaluation kit

**Useful outcome:** Synthetic test files, error rubric and repeatable evaluation workbook.

**Pause if:** Not certification of legal accuracy; tests must reflect actual use.

**First test:** Have two qualified reviewers score the same sample workflow.

**Capacity check:** $249 test price, 2 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-035) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
