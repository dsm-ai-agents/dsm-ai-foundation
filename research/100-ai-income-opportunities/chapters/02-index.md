## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Editing, positioning, campaign measurement and brand judgment**.

Potential buyer access: Marketing agency subcontracting, local business networks and targeted portfolio samples.

Evidence: Medium: paid adjacent work visible; packages and rates unvalidated. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-006: Interview-to-content retainer

**Useful outcome:** One recorded interview turned into four posts and one newsletter monthly.

**Pause if:** Crowded market; generic output is easy to replace.

**First test:** Sell one interview package and track approval time plus qualified replies.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 6 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-006) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-007: Customer case-study studio

**Useful outcome:** One approved customer case study with evidence table and sales summary.

**Pause if:** Customer permissions and every performance claim require verification.

**First test:** Build one case study from an authorized interview and obtain buyer approval.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 14 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-007) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-008: Ad concept testing pack

**Useful outcome:** Twelve concept drafts with three finished variants and a measurement plan.

**Pause if:** Ad budget is separate; do not promise sales or fabricate endorsements.

**First test:** Run a small buyer-funded test against the client's existing creative.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-008) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-009: Content accuracy cleanup

**Useful outcome:** Review fifty AI-written pages for unsupported claims and provide correction log.

**Pause if:** Domain knowledge needed; AI cannot verify its own claims reliably.

**First test:** Audit five pages and ask the agency to pay for the remaining batch.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 14 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-009) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-010: Local customer-question content pack

**Useful outcome:** Ten service FAQs, four short guides and a quarterly refresh list.

**Pause if:** Search ranking is not guaranteed; business must confirm pricing and service details.

**First test:** Use twenty real anonymized inquiries to create and sell one pack.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-010) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
