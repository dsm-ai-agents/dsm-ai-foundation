## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Teaching, learning assessment, subject knowledge and accessibility**.

Potential buyer access: Training providers, professional associations and established educator networks.

Evidence: Medium for training generally; individual educational products unvalidated. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-046: Corporate AI starter workshop

**Useful outcome:** Two-hour role-based workshop, exercises and follow-up checklist.

**Pause if:** Preparation and follow-up consume time; free tutorials compete.

**First test:** Sell one workshop with a before-and-after task assessment.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 16 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-046) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-047: Teacher lesson adaptation packs

**Useful outcome:** Editable lesson variants with source-aligned answer keys.

**Pause if:** Subject accuracy, copyright and student-data boundaries require review.

**First test:** Test one unit with three teachers before building the full pack.

**Capacity check:** $49 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-047) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-048: Course-to-microlearning conversion

**Useful outcome:** Ten short lessons from client-owned long-form material.

**Pause if:** Do not claim improved learning without evaluation.

**First test:** Convert one module and compare learner feedback.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 24 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-048) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-049: Tutor practice-bank builder

**Useful outcome:** One hundred reviewed questions with explanations and difficulty tags.

**Pause if:** No copying protected exams; answer keys need independent checks.

**First test:** Have subject experts review twenty items and test with consenting learners.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 18 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-049) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-050: Role-play training simulator

**Useful outcome:** Bounded practice scenarios with rubric-based facilitator review.

**Pause if:** Automated feedback can be wrong; trainers retain evaluation authority.

**First test:** Pilot three scenarios with one trainer and compare ratings.

**Capacity check:** $250 test price, 2 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-050) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
