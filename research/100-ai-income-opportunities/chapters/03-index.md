## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Catalog operations, spreadsheets, merchandising and customer-service processes**.

Potential buyer access: Shopify agency partners, merchant communities and local retailers.

Evidence: Medium: direct platform substitutes verified; differentiation essential. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-011: Catalog enrichment and QA

**Useful outcome:** Fifty corrected product records with attribute checks and brand-consistent copy.

**Pause if:** Basic generation is bundled free; charge for data quality and completion.

**First test:** Repair five SKUs and compare errors against the current catalog.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-011) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-012: Return-reason analysis service

**Useful outcome:** Monthly analysis of return notes with prioritized product and FAQ fixes.

**Pause if:** Small samples and mislabeled reasons can mislead; no automatic policy changes.

**First test:** Analyze a redacted month's returns and validate themes with staff.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 6 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-012) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-013: Customer-support knowledge cleanup

**Useful outcome:** Thirty approved help articles plus a fifty-question answer test.

**Pause if:** Built-in support tools compete; quality and handoff design are the offer.

**First test:** Clean one topic and measure correct answers on held-out questions.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-013) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-014: Supplier feed normalization

**Useful outcome:** Recurring import-ready catalog file with exceptions report.

**Pause if:** SKU mismatches can cause wrong shipments; validate before import.

**First test:** Process one supplier feed with exact-match field checks.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-014) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-015: Merchandising performance brief

**Useful outcome:** Monthly dashboard and narrative covering agreed inventory and sales metrics.

**Pause if:** Narrative is not a demand forecast; calculations must reconcile.

**First test:** Compare one brief with the owner's current reporting process.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 5 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-015) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
