## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Clear writing, domain expertise, instructional design and basic spreadsheet skills**.

Potential buyer access: Existing professional contacts, specialist newsletters and niche communities that permit offers.

Evidence: Medium: established delivery channels; individual products unvalidated. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-001: Role-specific workflow kit

**Useful outcome:** Ten tested workflows, sample inputs, output checks and editable templates.

**Pause if:** Generic prompt packs have many free substitutes; sell tested outcomes.

**First test:** Offer a three-workflow sample to five project managers and seek three paid preorders.

**Capacity check:** $49 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-001) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-002: Client onboarding template bundle

**Useful outcome:** Intake form, scope worksheet, kickoff agenda and handoff checklist.

**Pause if:** Templates need tailoring; no claim that contract text is legal advice.

**First test:** Have three consultants run one real onboarding using the prototype.

**Capacity check:** $79 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-002) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-003: Niche research briefing subscription

**Useful outcome:** Monthly curated brief with ten verified changes and practical implications.

**Pause if:** Free newsletters compete; source verification and unique expertise must justify price.

**First test:** Sell a four-week pilot to five readers and ask which decisions it changed.

**Capacity check:** $50 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-003) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-004: Spreadsheet explanation micro-product

**Useful outcome:** A workbook that checks uploaded non-sensitive sample data and explains formula results.

**Pause if:** AI must not invent totals; formulas and reconciliation remain authoritative.

**First test:** Test five messy sample workbooks with three bookkeepers.

**Capacity check:** $99 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-004) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-005: Profession-specific ChatGPT plugin kit

**Useful outcome:** Reusable skill instructions, reference examples, installation guide and acceptance checklist.

**Pause if:** Account permissions and platform rules change; no assumed directory payout.

**First test:** Test installation and report quality with three eligible users before selling.

**Capacity check:** $149 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-005) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
