## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-026: Client document chase system

**Useful outcome:** Missing-document tracker and approval-ready reminder drafts.

**Pause if:** Do not ingest confidential records into unapproved tools.

**First test:** Pilot with five consenting clients and count unresolved requests.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 5 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-026) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-027: Monthly close narrative service

**Useful outcome:** Management commentary grounded in reconciled financial statements.

**Pause if:** No unsupervised accounting conclusions or invented business explanations.

**First test:** Prepare one month and reconcile every number with the CFO.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-027) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-028: Receipt extraction exception desk

**Useful outcome:** Structured receipt file with duplicates and low-confidence fields flagged.

**Pause if:** Existing accounting software competes; never auto-post uncertain entries.

**First test:** Benchmark one hundred redacted receipts against manual entry.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-028) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-029: Accounting SOP documentation

**Useful outcome:** Five approved process guides from staff walkthroughs.

**Pause if:** Tax guidance and technical judgments require practitioner approval.

**First test:** Document one process and test with a new staff member.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-029) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-030: Audit evidence index preparation

**Useful outcome:** Searchable evidence inventory with source links and missing-item register.

**Pause if:** Administrative support only; no audit opinion or fabricated evidence.

**First test:** Pilot one non-sensitive evidence folder under auditor supervision.

**Capacity check:** $2,500 test price, 30 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-030) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
