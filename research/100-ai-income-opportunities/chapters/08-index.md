## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Practice operations, privacy-aware design and qualified clinical review when needed**.

Potential buyer access: Practice managers, existing healthcare IT vendors and professional referrals.

Evidence: Medium for administrative pain; clinical and data requirements raise entry barrier. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-036: Practice public FAQ refresh

**Useful outcome:** Twenty approved public-facing FAQs on logistics and services.

**Pause if:** Exclude personalized medical advice and patient data.

**First test:** Refresh five nonclinical FAQs and get manager approval.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-036) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-037: Referral packet completeness checker

**Useful outcome:** Missing-field report for staff review in an approved environment.

**Pause if:** Protected health information requires approved controls and agreements.

**First test:** Use synthetic packets first, then a practice-approved supervised pilot.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-037) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-038: Appointment communication review

**Useful outcome:** Draft-only reminder and rescheduling workflow with escalation rules.

**Pause if:** No emergency triage; consent, privacy and message delivery must be reviewed.

**First test:** Measure staff correction and escalation rates on a small approved pilot.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-038) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-039: Patient handout readability service

**Useful outcome:** Plain-language versions of ten clinician-provided handouts.

**Pause if:** Clinical review and qualified translation where relevant; no treatment changes.

**First test:** Have clinicians compare one revised handout to the original.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 16 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-039) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-040: Practice staff training micro-course

**Useful outcome:** Short course on approved AI use, data boundaries and checking outputs.

**Pause if:** Requires current organizational policy; not a compliance certification.

**First test:** Train one team and test handling of unsafe example prompts.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 18 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-040) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
