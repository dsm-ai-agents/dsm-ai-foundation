# Chapter 16: Insurance and financial-service operations

[← Chapter index](../CHAPTER-INDEX.md) · [Master decision index](../MASTER-INDEX.md)

## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Regulated-service operations, document accuracy and secure workflows**.

Potential buyer access: Licensed agency principals, compliance consultants and established industry vendors.

Evidence: Medium for sector AI use; qualified review and procurement essential. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-076: Insurance renewal preparation desk

**Useful outcome:** Missing-document list and source-linked renewal briefing.

**Pause if:** No coverage recommendations, underwriting decisions or binding changes.

**First test:** Pilot five files under a licensed agent's supervision.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 14 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-076) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-077: Insurance claims file organizer

**Useful outcome:** Indexed evidence packet with chronology and missing-item flags.

**Pause if:** No fraud verdicts, coverage decisions or fabricated loss details.

**First test:** Compare one historical file with a professional's existing packet.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-077) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-078: Financial advisor content review support

**Useful outcome:** Draft educational content plus claim/source checklist for approval.

**Pause if:** No individualized investment advice or unapproved performance claims.

**First test:** Prepare one newsletter through the firm's normal review process.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-078) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-079: Client meeting follow-up preparation

**Useful outcome:** Draft action list from approved meeting notes with human confirmation.

**Pause if:** Sensitive data and fiduciary obligations; no transaction execution.

**First test:** Shadow ten meetings in the firm's approved environment.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-079) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-080: Compliance evidence assembly

**Useful outcome:** Evidence inventory and status dashboard against a supplied checklist.

**Pause if:** Does not determine legal compliance or replace an audit.

**First test:** Assemble one checklist section for a compliance officer to review.

**Capacity check:** $2,500 test price, 30 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-080) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
