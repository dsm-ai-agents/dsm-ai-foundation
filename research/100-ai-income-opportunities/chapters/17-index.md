## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Research, CRM operations, interviewing and accurate business writing**.

Potential buyer access: Sales consultants, small B2B agencies and professional-network referrals.

Evidence: Medium: established work categories; specific buying intent unvalidated. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-081: Account research briefs

**Useful outcome:** Ten concise company briefs with cited facts and relevant questions.

**Pause if:** No private personal data scraping or invented buying intent.

**First test:** Test three briefs with sales staff before selling a batch.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-081) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-082: Proposal knowledge library

**Useful outcome:** Approved answer bank with source, owner and review date.

**Pause if:** Client confidentiality and stale claims require strict controls.

**First test:** Build one service-line library and compare proposal prep time.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 24 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-082) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-083: Customer interview synthesis

**Useful outcome:** Coded themes, supporting excerpts and unresolved contradictions.

**Pause if:** Small sample is not market sizing; preserve disagreement.

**First test:** Compare synthesis of five interviews with an experienced researcher.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-083) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-084: CRM data cleanup and enrichment

**Useful outcome:** Deduplicated account file with verified fields and change log.

**Pause if:** No unauthorized scraping; review merges before changing live CRM.

**First test:** Validate one hundred records and measure false merges.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 24 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-084) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-085: Public tender fit brief

**Useful outcome:** Source-linked tender shortlist and requirements matrix.

**Pause if:** Do not guarantee eligibility or awards; verify live deadlines.

**First test:** Evaluate five notices with a contracting professional.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-085) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
