## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Real-estate operations, accurate property data and professional editing**.

Potential buyer access: Brokerage administrators, property-management associations and existing agent referrals.

Evidence: Medium: sector adoption observed; buyer budgets vary. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-016: Verified listing launch pack

**Useful outcome:** Fact-checked listing description, brochure copy and five channel adaptations.

**Pause if:** No invented amenities, misleading imagery or discriminatory housing language.

**First test:** Sell one listing pack and record edits and turnaround.

**Capacity check:** $250 test price, 4 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-016) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-017: Property inbox triage setup

**Useful outcome:** Draft-only categorization and routing for maintenance and tenant emails.

**Pause if:** Emergency messages need reliable human escalation; tenant privacy matters.

**First test:** Run a two-week shadow pilot without sending replies automatically.

**Capacity check:** $2,500 test price, 30 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-017) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-018: Lease abstract preparation

**Useful outcome:** Structured dates and obligations table with page citations for ten leases.

**Pause if:** Legal interpretation stays with qualified professionals; missed dates are costly.

**First test:** Compare two abstracts against the manager's existing records.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 24 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-018) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-019: Tenant welcome micro-kit

**Useful outcome:** Editable move-in checklist, local-service directory and approved FAQ.

**Pause if:** No lease substitutes; local rules and contact details require updates.

**First test:** Sell a kit to three managers and observe actual use.

**Capacity check:** $79 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-019) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-020: Brokerage marketing workflow training

**Useful outcome:** Two sessions, approved examples and a reusable review checklist.

**Pause if:** Do not imply licensed advice or guaranteed leads; account access varies.

**First test:** Train one team on one workflow and assess output before and after.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-020) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
