## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Editing, visual judgment, sound/video quality control and language expertise**.

Potential buyer access: Podcast agencies, production studios, established creators and localization firms.

Evidence: Medium: adjacent paid creative work; crowded and rights-sensitive. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-086: Podcast repurposing service

**Useful outcome:** Four short clips, reviewed captions and one episode brief monthly.

**Pause if:** Rights and speaker consent required; AI-selected clips can distort context.

**First test:** Sell one episode package and record revision time.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-086) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-087: Product photo variation studio

**Useful outcome:** Ten labeled creative product scenes using authorized reference assets.

**Pause if:** No deceptive size or functionality changes; not a substitute for evidence photos.

**First test:** Deliver three proof images reviewed against actual product photos.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-087) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-088: Accessible caption cleanup

**Useful outcome:** Reviewed captions and transcript for one hour of video.

**Pause if:** Accessibility needs real review; automated captions alone are insufficient.

**First test:** Compare ten minutes against manual transcription for accuracy.

**Capacity check:** $250 test price, 5 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-088) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-089: Localization QA service

**Useful outcome:** Reviewed translation of a defined nonregulated content batch with glossary.

**Pause if:** Language expertise required; medical/legal material needs specialist review.

**First test:** Use a qualified bilingual reviewer on one sample page.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 16 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-089) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-090: Brand asset micro-library

**Useful outcome:** Coherent editable icon/background kit plus usage guide.

**Pause if:** Saturation, asset rights and inconsistent generations reduce value.

**First test:** Pre-sell one style pack to five target designers.

**Capacity check:** $49 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-090) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
