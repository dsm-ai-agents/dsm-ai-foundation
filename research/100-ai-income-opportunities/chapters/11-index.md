## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-051: Menu content accuracy pack

**Useful outcome:** Updated menu descriptions, digital listings and approved dietary wording.

**Pause if:** Never infer allergens or dietary safety; chef approves every claim.

**First test:** Refresh one menu section and verify with kitchen staff.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-051) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-052: Weekly menu performance brief

**Useful outcome:** Computed item-margin table and plain-language decision notes.

**Pause if:** Incomplete cost data misleads; AI must not invent ingredient costs.

**First test:** Analyze four weeks and review with owner and chef.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-052) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-053: Catering proposal assistant

**Useful outcome:** Draft proposals using approved packages, constraints and price tables.

**Pause if:** Binding prices and availability need staff confirmation.

**First test:** Pilot ten inquiries and compare preparation time and errors.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-053) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-054: Restaurant review insight service

**Useful outcome:** Monthly theme report from authorized reviews and draft response library.

**Pause if:** No fake reviews or manufactured praise; respect platform access rules.

**First test:** Analyze one month's reviews and identify a change the manager accepts.

**Capacity check:** $250 test price, 3 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-054) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-055: Kitchen onboarding content service

**Useful outcome:** Five staff-approved illustrated process guides and quizzes.

**Pause if:** Food safety procedures must come from qualified approved sources.

**First test:** Test one guide with staff and revise confusing steps.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-055) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
