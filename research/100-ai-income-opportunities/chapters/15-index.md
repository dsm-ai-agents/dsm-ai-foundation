## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Nonprofit operations, grant research, donor communication and evidence handling**.

Potential buyer access: Local nonprofit networks, existing grant consultants and association executives.

Evidence: Medium: named implementation example; budget constraints significant. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-071: Grant opportunity screening brief

**Useful outcome:** Monthly shortlist with eligibility, deadlines and original links.

**Pause if:** No invented grants or funding guarantees; verify each funder's live rules.

**First test:** Screen ten opportunities and validate eligibility with the nonprofit.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-071) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-072: Impact report production

**Useful outcome:** Annual impact narrative and visuals brief based on approved metrics.

**Pause if:** Do not fabricate outcomes, beneficiary stories or causal impact.

**First test:** Produce a two-page section reconciled to source records.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 26 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-072) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-073: Donor communication adaptation

**Useful outcome:** Four monthly audience-specific drafts from approved campaign material.

**Pause if:** Protect donor data; no automatic solicitation or manipulative claims.

**First test:** Test drafts with staff and a consenting donor advisory group.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 6 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-073) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-074: Volunteer onboarding assistant kit

**Useful outcome:** Editable FAQ, orientation script and coordinator checklist.

**Pause if:** Safeguarding and eligibility rules stay with the organization.

**First test:** Have three coordinators use the kit with new volunteers.

**Capacity check:** $99 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-074) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-075: Association member research digest

**Useful outcome:** Monthly cited digest of sector changes and member questions.

**Pause if:** Association may already have free sources; editorial expertise differentiates.

**First test:** Publish one internal pilot for editorial review and usefulness feedback.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-075) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
