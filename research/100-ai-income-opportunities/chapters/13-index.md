## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-061: Factory SOP digitization

**Useful outcome:** Five operator-reviewed digital work instructions from existing procedures.

**Pause if:** No invented machine settings or safety steps; qualified signoff required.

**First test:** Document one nonhazardous process and test with an operator.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 26 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-061) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-062: RFQ intake structuring

**Useful outcome:** Standardized request summary and missing-specification checklist.

**Pause if:** Drawings and tolerances need technical verification; no automated quote.

**First test:** Shadow twenty RFQs and compare fields with estimator review.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-062) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-063: Quality report narrative service

**Useful outcome:** Monthly report from verified defect counts and inspection logs.

**Pause if:** AI suggestions are hypotheses, not root-cause proof or product-release approval.

**First test:** Reconcile one report with source counts and manager interpretation.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-063) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-064: Auto repair estimate explainer

**Useful outcome:** Customer-friendly explanations of technician-approved estimates.

**Pause if:** No vehicle diagnosis, repair recommendation or safety assurance by AI.

**First test:** Have technicians approve twenty drafts before customer use.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 6 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-064) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-065: Supplier document index

**Useful outcome:** Searchable index of certificates, manuals and expiry dates.

**Pause if:** No certification of supplier compliance; verify original documents.

**First test:** Index fifty documents and test exact retrieval and dates.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-065) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
