## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Trade workflow familiarity, document control and automation testing**.

Potential buyer access: Local trade associations, contractor referrals and existing office-software consultants.

Evidence: Medium: operational need supported; technical reliability is decisive. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-021: Estimate follow-up workflow

**Useful outcome:** Approved reminder drafts and a dashboard of open estimates.

**Pause if:** Use consented contacts; no invented quotes or automatic commitments.

**First test:** Shadow twenty estimates and compare staff follow-up completion.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 5 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-021) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-022: Jobsite voice-note reports

**Useful outcome:** Reviewed daily report from crew voice notes and photos.

**Pause if:** Safety incidents require immediate existing procedures, not delayed AI summaries.

**First test:** Produce reports for one crew for one week and verify every event.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-022) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-023: Bid document checklist service

**Useful outcome:** Requirement matrix with page citations, deadlines and missing items.

**Pause if:** Do not guarantee bid completeness or provide engineering judgments.

**First test:** Review one small bid against an estimator's manual checklist.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-023) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-024: Home-service FAQ assistant setup

**Useful outcome:** Website assistant limited to approved services and human handoff.

**Pause if:** Native website tools compete; no binding prices or unsafe instructions.

**First test:** Test fifty realistic questions including pricing and emergency edge cases.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 26 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-024) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-025: Maintenance-plan communication kit

**Useful outcome:** Editable seasonal reminders, service-plan explanations and renewal messages.

**Pause if:** No exaggerated savings or unsupported warranty claims.

**First test:** Ask three operators to buy and use one seasonal campaign.

**Capacity check:** $99 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-025) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
