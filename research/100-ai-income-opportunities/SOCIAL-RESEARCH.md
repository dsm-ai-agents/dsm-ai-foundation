## Themes and counterevidence

1. **Sell a specific improvement.** Reddit contributors repeatedly describe owner interest in fewer administrative steps rather than AI as an abstract service. This supports testing concrete offers; it does not prove a conversion rate.
2. **Building is not selling.** One poster reports months of learning and outreach without a customer. We retained this negative case rather than collecting only success stories.
3. **Maintenance changes the economics.** Another discussion raises fragile workflows and review burdens. Recurring fees should correspond to genuine ongoing work, not obscure an unstable installation.
4. **Coordination is a business problem.** The LinkedIn discussion raises shared context, authority and team processes. This suggests training and workflow design opportunities, but remains expert interpretation.
5. **Public founder success is selection-biased.** An exceptional founder with an established audience is not a representative beginner. The catalog does not use celebrity revenue to forecast reader income.

None of these themes establishes that chatter is “massive.” We did not collect a defensible platform-wide denominator, engagement time series, geographic sample or unique-author count.

## Excluded or downgraded material

- Automated X trending summaries, including founder revenue summaries: not direct original evidence.
- Anonymous SaaS-for-sale revenue assertions: seller claims, not independently verified accounts.
- Posts advertising a paid course or community using large revenue claims: useful only as marketing examples unless independently corroborated.
- Numerical conversion-rate advice in Reddit comments: no verified denominator or supporting dataset.
- An inaccessible SHRM regional page shell: did not establish report contents; the separate policy-document excerpt is labeled accordingly.

## Reusable social research protocol

For a future refresh, choose a buyer niche before searching. Capture the original URL, exact date when available, author role if verifiable, problem described, current workaround, evidence of spending, objections, and any promotion or affiliate link. Include positive, negative and neutral examples. Deduplicate reposts and repeated creators.

Search buyer communities as well as builder communities. Builder discussion can overstate purchasing demand because participants are often selling to each other. Seek first-person buyer accounts, then check the claimed workflow against vendor documentation or industry research. Do not contact anyone or publish replies without a separate instruction.
