# 100 AI Income Opportunities

**A research project within DSM AI Foundation**  
Research date: **7 November 2026** · Market: **United States** · Currency: **USD**

## Project intention

Help working professionals turn existing knowledge and AI capabilities into specific products, micro-products, plugins and services that solve real customer problems. Explore paths toward **$1,000–$10,000 in monthly gross revenue**, without presenting those outcomes as guaranteed or typical.

The project includes accessible starting points and more advanced opportunities across 20 industry and buyer groups. “Beginner” refers to technical entry difficulty, not permission to perform work requiring professional competence or licensing.

The user requested broad research—including Reddit, X/Twitter, LinkedIn and credible influential practitioners—followed by a saved catalog of 100 ideas and a plan for developing them into images. Publishing strategy is outside this phase. Images have not been generated.

## Current status

- Research synthesis and a 100-idea working catalog are saved.
- There are 32 source/access records, including primary industry research, platform documentation, named practitioners and public discussions.
- Each idea has a buyer, deliverable, AI role, skill level, price hypothesis, revenue arithmetic, delivery-hours assumption, acquisition route, validation test and constraint.
- No customer interviews, paid pilots, independently verified creator earnings or willingness-to-pay tests have been completed.
- X coverage is limited by 403 responses; indexed content is explicitly separated from readable evidence.
- Some industry groups have only indirect supporting evidence and are marked exploratory.
- Source rates and dates are a snapshot; recheck before commercial or public use.

## Editorial promise

Sell a defined outcome, show the human expertise required, expose the economics, and make uncertainty visible. Do not use “anyone can earn,” “passive income,” “guaranteed $10k,” or unverified revenue screenshots as evidence.
