# 100 AI income opportunities

Research snapshot: **7 November 2026**. Target buyers: United States. All amounts: USD.

These are **100 distinct offer hypotheses**, not 100 verified profitable businesses. Sources identify adjacent demand, workflows, substitutes or constraints. No source verifies the proposed prices or the income of a typical beginner. Read [research findings](RESEARCH.md), [economics](ECONOMICS.md), and [source details](SOURCES.md) before using a card publicly.

## How to read the cards

- **Beginner:** low technical barrier, but needs the relevant professional skills and human review. It does not mean anyone can perform regulated work.
- **Intermediate:** domain experience plus structured workflows, testing and competent delivery.
- **Advanced:** engineering or specialist review, sensitive data controls, integration reliability and ongoing support.
- **Download/project/workshop:** each unit is a new sale or engagement in that month. These are not recurring subscriptions.
- **Retainer/subscription:** each unit is an active paying customer that month. Renewal and retention are unproven.
- All prices and hours are planning assumptions. Monthly revenue is gross, before expenses and tax. Unit counts round up to meet the stated target; actual revenue can exceed it.
- Delivery/support hours exclude initial product development, onboarding, selling, learning, administration and revisions beyond the defined scope. The economics file adds capacity checks. High-volume digital sales require distribution; low delivery effort does not imply easy acquisition.
- Source IDs refer to context, not validation of the exact offer. Lower-confidence sectors are explicitly marked.
