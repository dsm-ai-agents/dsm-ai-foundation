## Slide 6: What We Learned
- Title: Four things V2 taught us
  - 01 New joiners every week: New and existing members move at different speeds.
  - 02 Members stay for the long run: One-time fee, 3+ months typical, some over a year.
  - 03 Weekly hands-on drains energy: Fast market shifts, constant rewrites, communication gaps.
  - 04 One pace fits no one: Some need basics, some variety, some advanced.
- Notes: Fixed learning pathways were tried and failed because the market moved too fast.
