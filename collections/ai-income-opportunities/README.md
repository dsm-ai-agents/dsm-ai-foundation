# AI Income Opportunities — 100 opportunities

Part of [Data Science Masterminds Research Studio](../../README.md). **Program:** Agentic Founder. **Authors:** Kunaal Naik & Arpan Saxena.

Help professionals identify a useful AI-enabled product or service, understand its buyer and delivery requirements, and choose a small commercial test. This collection covers 100 opportunities across 20 industry groups. Research is a United States/USD snapshot dated 7 November 2026; the wider studio can cover other markets and subjects.

## Current state

Research and visuals are complete for the initial brief: 100 topic images, 20 chapter introductions, and one master index. Detailed articles and web publishing are planned. The research contains 32 source/access records; access limits and exploratory evidence are retained. Prices, revenue arithmetic, and delivery capacity are hypotheses, not observed or guaranteed earnings. Customer interviews and paid validation have not been completed.

## Rebuild the gallery

From the repository root:

```bash
python collections/ai-income-opportunities/production/build_gallery.py --require-complete
```

Image files and source IDs are preserved. Future downloadable packages belong in `exports/`; generated ZIPs are excluded from Git.
