## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Software engineering, authentication, evaluation, deployment and support**.

Potential buyer access: Existing software agencies, professional referrals and specialized developer communities.

Evidence: Medium: paid technical demand and platform mechanics observed; individual offers unvalidated. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-096: Custom GPT-to-plugin migration service

**Useful outcome:** Inventory, migration, replacement integration plan and regression checks.

**Pause if:** Workspace-specific permissions; custom actions may need rebuilding.

**First test:** Migrate one authorized noncritical workflow and verify end-user access.

**Capacity check:** $2,500 test price, 30 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-096) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-097: Niche SaaS support integration

**Useful outcome:** Source-grounded support assistant with evaluations and human escalation.

**Pause if:** Native support products compete; monitor hallucinations and access boundaries.

**First test:** Run a shadow pilot on fifty historical questions before production.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 14 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-097) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-098: AI workflow evaluation service

**Useful outcome:** Fifty-case test set, failure taxonomy and prioritized remediation report.

**Pause if:** No blanket certification; test coverage and human gold labels limit conclusions.

**First test:** Audit one narrowly scoped workflow against buyer-agreed criteria.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 26 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-098) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-099: Internal reporting plugin integration

**Useful outcome:** Read-only connector and draft weekly status report from approved systems.

**Pause if:** Permissions, prompt injection and confidential data boundaries need engineering.

**First test:** Test one team and three report cycles before extending scope.

**Capacity check:** $3,000 test price, 36 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-099) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-100: Single-workflow micro-SaaS

**Useful outcome:** A hosted brief-to-deliverable tracker with structured AI draft assistance.

**Pause if:** Customer acquisition, churn, security and support dominate; generic wrappers are fragile.

**First test:** Sell three concierge pilots before writing a multi-tenant product.

**Capacity check:** $100 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-100) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
