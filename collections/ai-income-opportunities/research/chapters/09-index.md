# Chapter 09: Recruiting and human resources

[← Chapter index](../CHAPTER-INDEX.md) · [Master decision index](../MASTER-INDEX.md)

## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **HR operations, structured interviewing, privacy and inclusive writing**.

Potential buyer access: Independent recruiters, HR consultants and regional employer associations.

Evidence: Medium for adjacent workflows; no automated employment decisions. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-041: Job description clarity service

**Useful outcome:** Five role descriptions with hiring-manager-approved requirements.

**Pause if:** Do not invent duties or use protected characteristics as selection criteria.

**First test:** Rewrite one role and compare with manager's actual expectations.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-041) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-042: Recruiter interview note organizer

**Useful outcome:** Structured, source-linked notes from consented interviews.

**Pause if:** No candidate ranking or automated hire/reject decisions.

**First test:** Compare ten summaries with recruiter review and correction logs.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 10 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-042) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-043: New-hire onboarding assistant

**Useful outcome:** Approved-policy FAQ with citations and escalation.

**Pause if:** Access permissions and policy currency matter; no legal interpretation.

**First test:** Run fifty questions including outdated-policy traps.

**Capacity check:** $2,500 test price, 30 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-043) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-044: HR process microlearning packs

**Useful outcome:** Reusable five-lesson packs on approved internal processes.

**Pause if:** Employer-specific policies still need review; not mandatory certified training.

**First test:** Sell one pack to three consultants and observe learner completion.

**Capacity check:** $149 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-044) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-045: Employee survey synthesis

**Useful outcome:** Anonymized theme report and evidence-backed action questions.

**Pause if:** Re-identification and fabricated consensus risks; retain dissenting views.

**First test:** Analyze one survey with minimum group-size rules agreed in advance.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 18 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-045) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
