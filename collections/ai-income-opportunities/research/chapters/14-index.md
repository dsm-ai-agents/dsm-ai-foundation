## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Freight operations, spreadsheets, document reconciliation and exception handling**.

Potential buyer access: Freight broker networks, small 3PL operators and warehouse software partners.

Evidence: Exploratory to medium: adjacent paid work; exact products unvalidated. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-066: Proof-of-delivery document desk

**Useful outcome:** Indexed delivery documents with missing-record and discrepancy report.

**Pause if:** Wrong shipment matching can delay payment; no autonomous claims.

**First test:** Process fifty completed shipments with human reconciliation.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 12 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-066) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-067: Freight invoice exception checker

**Useful outcome:** Matched invoice table with disputed lines flagged for review.

**Pause if:** Tariff and accessorial interpretation require expert review.

**First test:** Benchmark one historical batch against accountant decisions.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 14 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-067) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-068: Customer shipment update drafts

**Useful outcome:** Draft status messages grounded in current dispatch data.

**Pause if:** No invented ETAs or promises; delayed source systems limit accuracy.

**First test:** Shadow one customer account without automatic sends.

**Capacity check:** $750 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-068) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-069: Warehouse training content kit

**Useful outcome:** Five process-specific training modules and checklists.

**Pause if:** Safety and equipment instructions require qualified review.

**First test:** Test one approved module with a supervisor and new hire.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-069) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-070: Wholesale product-data translation

**Useful outcome:** Reviewed bilingual product data for fifty SKUs.

**Pause if:** Technical claims and regulated labels need specialist review.

**First test:** Have a qualified bilingual reviewer check ten representative SKUs.

**Capacity check:** $2,000 test price, 26 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-070) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
