## Assess your fit

Consider this chapter if you already bring: **Rural business operations, recordkeeping, seasonal planning and local knowledge**.

Potential buyer access: Producer associations, farm business advisors and existing local supplier relationships.

Evidence: Exploratory: indirect context only; validate with actual operators first. These are offer hypotheses, not verified earnings.

## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-091: Farm record organization service

**Useful outcome:** Structured expense and activity records from supplied notes.

**Pause if:** No tax advice or inferred farm events; seasonal budgets constrain demand.

**First test:** Organize one month's records and reconcile with receipts.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-091) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-092: CSA customer communication pack

**Useful outcome:** Weekly box notes, crop descriptions and approved pickup reminders.

**Pause if:** Seasonal revenue; no invented availability or food-safety claims.

**First test:** Run a four-week paid pilot with one CSA.

**Capacity check:** $250 test price, 4 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-092) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-093: Farm-stay marketing launch kit

**Useful outcome:** Verified visitor FAQ, listing copy and seasonal event descriptions.

**Pause if:** Verify access, safety, permits and accessibility with the operator.

**First test:** Sell one attraction's launch pack and track inquiry quality.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-093) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-094: Equipment manual search index

**Useful outcome:** Source-linked manual index for a defined equipment set.

**Pause if:** No autonomous repair or hazardous procedure instructions.

**First test:** Test fifty queries with a qualified technician.

**Capacity check:** $2,500 test price, 30 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-094) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-095: Producer grant application organizer

**Useful outcome:** Eligibility checklist, evidence index and draft narrative from verified facts.

**Pause if:** No grant guarantees or invented eligibility; seasonal deadlines matter.

**First test:** Prepare one small application section for advisor review.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 16 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-095) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
