## Assess each option before deciding

### AI-056: Guest guide micro-product

**Useful outcome:** Editable arrival guide, house rules and verified local recommendations.

**Pause if:** Stale access details and local information need regular owner updates.

**First test:** Sell three guides and observe which guest questions remain.

**Capacity check:** $79 test price, 1 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-056) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-057: Hotel guest-message knowledge setup

**Useful outcome:** Approved response library and escalation map for front-desk staff.

**Pause if:** No unverified bookings, rates or accessibility promises.

**First test:** Run a one-week draft-only test at one property.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-057) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-058: Travel itinerary research service

**Useful outcome:** Source-linked itinerary options with current checks and alternatives.

**Pause if:** Advisor verifies live availability, entry rules and safety; no guarantees.

**First test:** Prepare one itinerary for an advisor using verified opening and booking data.

**Capacity check:** $500 test price, 8 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-058) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-059: Event proposal and run-sheet studio

**Useful outcome:** Proposal deck outline, supplier comparison and staff-approved run sheet.

**Pause if:** Vendor availability and all costs need confirmation.

**First test:** Support one small event and compare coordination errors.

**Capacity check:** $1,000 test price, 14 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-059) for revenue scenarios and sources.

### AI-060: Conference recap package

**Useful outcome:** Session summaries, newsletter and key-point graphics copy.

**Pause if:** Recording and reuse rights required; preserve speaker meaning.

**First test:** Produce one session recap and obtain speaker/organizer approval.

**Capacity check:** $1,500 test price, 20 assumed delivery/support hours per unit, before selling and setup. Read the [full topic](../100-IDEAS.md#ai-060) for revenue scenarios and sources.

## Make one decision

Pick the topic for which you have the strongest existing skills and buyer access. Compare the current workaround, make a small sample and ask for a paid pilot. If the buyer already has an adequate bundled feature, change the offer. Record the outcome in the [validation plan](../PLAN.md); check [economics](../ECONOMICS.md) before committing your time.
