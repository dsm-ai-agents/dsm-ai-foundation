## Proposed palette

| Role | Color | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep ink | #142823 | Main text and dark cover backgrounds |
| Evergreen | #146B50 | Opportunity/category accents |
| Warm ivory | #FAF7EF | Reading background |
| Amber | #F4B544 | Small emphasis blocks and arithmetic highlights |
| Muted slate | #52625D | Secondary text |
| White | #FFFFFF | Text on sufficiently dark backgrounds |

Use dark text on ivory or amber. Validate actual contrast at final font size; these choices are not a completed accessibility test. Avoid tiny white text on amber.

## Tone and composition

Practical, confident and grounded in professional work. Show recognizable customer workflows and useful deliverables. Avoid luxury imagery, piles of money, exaggerated earnings badges or generic robot illustrations.

Provisional layout: large idea number; short outcome title; buyer and entry tag; one workflow illustration; compact offer details; explicit “illustrative gross revenue” line; source/version footer. Use icons and labels together, not color alone.

Typography: Space Grotesk-like headings and Inter-like body, matching the parent series. Footer: Data Science Masterminds | Agentic Founder | Kunaal Naik & Arpan Saxena. Use deep evergreen #142823 background, ivory/white text, and amber #F4B544 accents.

## Information hierarchy for an idea card

1. AI-### and industry.
2. The customer outcome, in plain language.
3. Who buys and why.
4. What the professional delivers.
5. Beginner/intermediate/advanced with a short prerequisite.
6. Price hypothesis and one transparent multiplication.
7. First validation step.
8. Main constraint and source reference.

Suggested copy example for AI-011:

> Catalog cleanup for specialty retailers  
> Turn incomplete product records into a checked, consistent catalog.  
> Test offer: 50 SKUs for $750.  
> Illustration: 2 projects/month = $1,500 gross, before costs.  
> Start by fixing 5 SKUs and asking a merchant to buy the full batch.  
> Built-in AI copy is already available; accuracy and completion are the value.

This is original editorial copy describing a hypothetical offer, not a testimonial.

## Production sequence

1. Reconcile this brief with the parent visual guide.
2. Design one cover and five card layouts.
3. Review exact text, numerical labels, contrast and small-screen readability.
4. Generate illustrations without embedding essential small text; typeset the approved text separately when practical.
5. Export editable source, final images and a manifest keyed to idea IDs.
6. Produce remaining cards in reviewed batches.

Canvas: landscape 16:9, matching the parent teaching library. Record actual native dimensions. Final PNGs are flattened images; generation prompts remain editable source specifications, not editable slide layers.
