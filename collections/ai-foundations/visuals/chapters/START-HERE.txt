HOW TO USE
1. Extract the entire ZIP into your preferred local or OneDrive folder.
2. Keep gallery.html and all ch01–ch12 folders together.
3. Open gallery.html in your browser to browse or search the images.
4. INDEX.md provides a text index with clickable image links.
5. Open a chapter folder: its chapter index comes first, then concepts in numerical order.
6. In PowerPoint, insert the PNGs as pictures. These files have flattened text and artwork.
7. For offline OneDrive access, select “Always keep on this device”.

ABOUT THE FILES
The PNGs preserve their native generated resolution, recorded in chapter manifests.
They are approximately 16:9, often 1672 × 941; they are not upscaled 1920 × 1080 files.
Each chapter README contains teaching qualifications and research references.
Business examples are illustrative, not claims about measured results or guaranteed income.

These images use the approved APEX design. Separate Master Class and Founder brand
variants, and editable PowerPoint layouts, are not included in this collection.
