## Teaching notes

These are original teaching examples informed by the DSM program brief, not measured business outcomes or promises of revenue. Customer discovery and prioritization are iterative. MVP means a credible test of value, not permission to omit essential quality or safety. The scorecard in 086 is explicitly illustrative. Relevant outreach still requires appropriate consent and lawful data handling. The unit economics example shows contribution before fixed costs and taxes, not net profit. A productized service still needs validation of demand, scope, delivery quality, and ongoing cost.
